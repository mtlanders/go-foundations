=============================================================
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
=============================================================
- No shared state between independently created watchers.
- High updates correctly on true, does not update on false.
- Loop variable is directly captured by each closure — not

- Loop-created watchers are independent under 1.21 semantics.
- Every closure-relevant decision justified in comments.
